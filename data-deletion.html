Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 2026

Laith Press respects your privacy and allows customers to request deletion of personal information associated with their customer service interactions.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your personal information, contact Laith Press through one of our official customer service channels and state that you would like your personal data to be deleted.

Please include enough information for us to identify your records, such as:

  • Your name.
  • The phone number used to communicate with Laith Press.
  • A brief description of the relevant conversation or order, if applicable.

Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on your request and applicable requirements, this may include:

  • Customer contact information.
  • Customer service conversation records.
  • Printing request and quotation information.
  • Uploaded images, documents, or files associated with your request.
  • Other personal information associated with your customer profile.

Processing Your Request

We will review valid deletion requests and process them within a reasonable period of time.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate business purposes.

Verification

We may ask you to verify that you are the owner of the phone number or account associated with the information before completing a deletion request.

Contact

Please contact Laith Press through its official business communication channels to submit a data deletion request or ask questions regarding your data.
