Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Laith Press collects, uses, and protects information when customers communicate with our customer service channels, including WhatsApp and other supported messaging platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Messages and inquiries sent to our customer service channels.
  • Printing order details such as product type, quantity, size, material, finishing, and delivery requirements.
  • Images, documents, artwork, or other files you send for printing or quotation purposes.
  • Information related to quotations, orders, payment status, and customer service requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to customer inquiries.
  • Prepare quotations and process printing orders.
  • Provide customer support and order updates.
  • Improve our products and customer service.
  • Maintain customer and order records where appropriate.

3. Automated and AI-Assisted Customer Service

Laith Press may use automated systems and artificial intelligence tools to assist with customer service, understand customer requests, retrieve relevant product information, and prepare responses.

Some requests, including payment, pricing, discount, or other business decisions, may be referred to a human representative for review.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be processed by service providers supporting communication, hosting, automation, artificial intelligence, and business operations where necessary.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

Customer information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain order records, resolve inquiries, and meet legitimate business or legal requirements.

7. Your Rights

Customers may contact Laith Press to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information where applicable.

8. WhatsApp and Meta

Communications through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp and Meta's applicable privacy policies and terms.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Laith Press through its official business communication channels.
